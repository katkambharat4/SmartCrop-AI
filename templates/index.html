Where Do Input Values Come From?

  • Soil Nutrients (N, P, K): From a laboratory soil test or your government Soil Health Card. Never guess soil nutrient values.
  • Temperature: From local meteorological reports or local thermometer readings.
  • Relative Humidity: From local weather bulletins or hygrometer data.
  • Rainfall: From local seasonal rainfall data or agricultural department bulletins.
  • Cultivation Season: Your planned agricultural cropping window (Kharif, Rabi, or Zaid).
⚠️ Entering a location does not automatically measure or fabricate soil N/P/K values.
👥

Who Can Use SmartCrop AI?

SmartCrop AI is built with barrier-free access in mind:

  • Individual Farmers: Any farmer with a smartphone, tablet, or PC can enter their values directly.
  • Assisted Access: If a farmer does not own a smartphone, an agriculture extension officer, village field worker, family member, or Common Service Center (CSC / KVK) can enter the field values on the farmer's behalf.
  • Students & Researchers: Useful for agricultural science and machine learning demonstrations.
✨ No smartphone required for every farmer. No login or registration needed.
⚠️

Important Real-World Limitations

  • User-Provided Inputs: SmartCrop AI currently relies on user-provided values. The current version does not connect to real-time IoT hardware.
  • No Automated Nutrient Sensing: Field location does not automatically determine soil N, P, or K. Soil test records must be used.
  • Soil Moisture: The model does not use soil moisture because it was not an available feature in the benchmark dataset.
  • Decision-Support Advisory: Recommendations are advisory and do not guarantee cultivation outcomes. Microclimatic and market factors should be considered before final planting.

About SmartCrop AI

SmartCrop AI was developed as a B.Tech Computer Science capstone project focusing on applied Machine Learning in precision agriculture. The project emphasizes transparent classification, reproducible ML pipelines, dual-tier input validation, and farmer-accessible web technologies without unnecessary cloud or database complexity.
