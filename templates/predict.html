Crop Recommendation Form

Enter agricultural and environmental values to receive an AI-assisted crop recommendation.

📖

Where do I get these values?

  • Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P), Potassium (K): Obtain from a recent soil test laboratory report or your government Soil Health Card. Do not guess these values.
  • Temperature: Obtain from local meteorological reports, weather forecasts, or a thermometer.
  • Relative Humidity: Obtain from local weather information or hygrometer measurements.
  • Rainfall: Obtain from local seasonal rain gauge reports or regional meteorological data.
  • Season: Select your current or planned cultivation cycle (Kharif, Rabi, Zaid, etc.).

⚠️ Important: Entering a location does not automatically supply soil N, P, or K values. The system does not currently connect to physical soil sensors.

👥

Who can use SmartCrop AI?

SmartCrop AI is designed to be accessible to all members of the farming community:

  • Individual Farmers: Any farmer with a smartphone, tablet, or home computer can enter their field numbers directly.
  • Assisted Access: If a farmer does not own a smartphone, an agriculture extension officer, local field assistant, family member, or Common Service Center (CSC / KVK) can enter the field values on the farmer's behalf.

💡 No account registration or login is required. Anyone can run a crop advisory immediately.
